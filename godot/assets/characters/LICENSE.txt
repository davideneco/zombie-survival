Origin : ORIGINAL work, generated from scratch for this project by tools/build-characters.py and the modules in tools/charlib/ (Blender 5.2 script,
v0.31.0). No external model, texture, scan, photo or animation file is read or downloaded:
  - shapes : signed distance fields written in the scripts (ellipsoids, round cones, rounded boxes, tori, surfaces of revolution, smooth unions,
    offsets for the clothes, displacements for the folds), meshed with the OpenVDB library bundled with Blender, decimated by Blender;
  - textures : computed per texel by tools/charlib/tex.py (value noise, fractal noise, cellular noise, stitches, camouflage, fabric, leather,
    skin, eyes) plus normals and ambient occlusion baked by Blender Cycles from the dense meshes generated above; the hair strands texture
    is drawn by tools/charlib/hair.py;
  - skeleton (57 bones), skin weights and the 10 animations (Idle, Walk, Run, Sprint, Sit_Moto, Sit_Bike, Sit_Tram, Sit_Tram_Drive, Death,
    Jump) are written in tools/charlib/skel.py, rig.py and anims.py.
The v0.21 low-poly models (previous tools/build-characters.py, in the git history) were also original; they are replaced, not reused.

Licence : same as the rest of the project (the repository owner's licence). Nothing in these files comes from Quaternius, Kenney, Poly Haven,
ambientCG or any other pack.

Originality of the designs: the 8 names and accent colours come from the browser game (src/characters.js), and the outfits are loosely
inspired by the game's primitive avatars (colours, accessories), but the designs are NOT reproductions of any copyrighted character:
  - marc    : soldier (generic woodland-like camouflage made by noise, helmet with a night-vision mount, plate carrier with pouches, backpack
              with a rolled mat, knee pads, gloves, boots).
  - lea     : nurse (blue scrubs, open white lab coat with pockets, pens and badge, stethoscope, red headband, red ponytail, blue nitrile
              gloves, green medical bag with a white cross, red-cross armband).
  - karim   : firefighter (orange turnout coat and dark trousers with reflective bands, yellow helmet with a raised visor and a neck flap,
              breathing apparatus with a cylinder and hose, axe on the back, leather gloves, short beard).
  - chloe   : student (quilted red parka, knitted scarf, headphones round the neck, jeans, sneakers, light-blue backpack, long dark hair).
  - lucian  : "the Purifier" (long black leather coat with white trims, gold epaulettes and buttons, hood, rubber respirator with two
              filter cartridges and a valve, round goggles, glowing relic).
  - picsou  : billionaire. The browser game's avatar is an anthropomorphic duck with a top hat; to stay clear of the protected cartoon
              character, the port uses an ORIGINAL design: a short stout elderly human tycoon (red tailcoat, gold waistcoat, cravat,
              top hat, monocle, white moustache, spats, gold cane). Only the name, the red/white/gold palette and the top hat come from the game.
  - klukai  : tactical doll (black bodysuit with doll-joint seams, cropped purple jacket with cyan piping, harness, holster, porcelain knee
              caps, knee boots, pale-blue hair in a high ponytail with a side-swept fringe).
  - harry   : sorcerer. Original design: midnight-blue robe with bell sleeves, floppy pointed hat with a gold band, round glasses, teal and
              gold striped scarf, gold sash, wand. No scar, no house colours, no uniform from any film or game.
No model, texture or sound was extracted from any game or film.
