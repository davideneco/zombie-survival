Characters of the Godot port (marc, lea, karim, chloe, lucian, picsou, klukai, harry) - assets/characters/*.glb

Origin : ORIGINAL work, modelled from scratch for this project by tools/build-characters.py (Blender 5.2 script, no external model, texture or animation
file is read: every mesh is generated from ellipsoids, tubes, rounded boxes and surfaces of revolution; the skeleton and the 8 animations (Idle, Walk, Run,
Sit_Moto, Sit_Bike, Sit_Tram, Sit_Tram_Drive, Death) are written in the same script, bone rotations only). Colours are plain PBR material values, no image.

Licence : same as the rest of the project (the repository owner's licence). Nothing in these files comes from Quaternius, Kenney or any other pack: the
low-poly cartoon look is only meant to sit well next to the Quaternius zombies (assets/zombies, CC0, see ../zombies/LICENSE.txt), which are NOT reused here.
The earlier idea of adapting "Ultimate Modular Men / Women" (Quaternius) was dropped: the pack page and the QAL licence note (see ../zombies/LICENSE.txt)
make redistribution of derived models uncertain, and building from scratch avoids the question.

Originality of the designs: the 8 names and accent colours come from the browser game (src/characters.js), and the outfits are loosely inspired by the game's
primitive avatars (colours, accessories), but the designs are NOT reproductions of any copyrighted character:
  - marc    : soldier (helmet with night-vision goggles, tactical vest, backpack with a rolled mat, knee pads).
  - lea     : nurse (white coat over blue scrubs, red headband, stethoscope, green medical bag with a cross).
  - karim   : firefighter (orange jacket with reflective bands, yellow helmet with neck flap, air tanks, axe on the back).
  - chloe   : student (red parka, cream scarf, headphones, light-blue backpack, long dark hair).
  - lucian  : "the Purifier" (long black coat with white lining and gold epaulettes, white full-face mask, glowing relic).
  - picsou  : billionaire. The browser game's avatar is an anthropomorphic duck with a top hat; to stay clear of the protected cartoon character, the port
              uses an ORIGINAL design: a short stout elderly human tycoon (red frock coat, black top hat, monocle, white moustache, gold cane). Only the name,
              the red/white/gold palette and the top hat come from the game.
  - klukai  : tactical doll (black cap, long pale-blue ponytail, purple cropped jacket, cyan straps, porcelain joints at knees and elbows).
  - harry   : sorcerer. Original design: dark midnight robe, floppy pointed hat with a gold band, round glasses, teal scarf, gold sash, wand. No scar, no house
              colours, no uniform from any film or game.
No model, texture or sound was extracted from any game or film.
